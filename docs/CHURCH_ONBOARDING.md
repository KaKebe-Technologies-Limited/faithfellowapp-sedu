# Church onboarding guide

Welcome to FaithFellows! This guide walks a church administrator (usually the pastor, church secretary or media lead) from application to a first live service. Most churches are fully set up in **one afternoon**.

**Need help?** Open a ticket under **Church admin → Support**, call 0393249845, or email service@kakebe.tech.

---

## Step 1: Create your account and apply

1. Go to **faithfellows.com → Register your church**.
2. Create a personal account (or sign in), then confirm your email address.
3. Fill in the application: church name, denomination, city/district, address, pastor's name, phone and a short description.
4. Submit. Our team reviews applications within one working day and may call to confirm details.

While you wait, you can already start the setup wizard. Your public page goes live once the church is approved.

## Step 2: The setup wizard (Church admin → Setup)

| Step | What to do | Tip |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Branding** | Upload a logo (square, 512×512) and a cover photo (1600×600). Pick your brand colour. | Use a photo of your congregation worshipping, not an empty building. |
| **Location** | Drop the pin on the map. | This powers "churches near me" and geofence check-in. |
| **Contact** | Phone, email, website and social links (Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp…). | |
| **Service times** | Add each service: day, time, language, location. | Members see these on your page and website. |
| **Team** | Invite staff by email and choose their role. | Start with 2–3 people. |

## Step 3: Roles and permissions

Under **Settings → Staff & roles**, every church gets preset roles: **Editor**, **Finance**, **Media**, **Events** and **Moderator**. You can edit them or create your own from the permission catalogue:

- *Content:* sermons, devotionals, announcements, reading plans, media library, live streams
- *Events:* events, RSVPs, tickets, check-in
- *Finance:* view giving, manage giving (cash entries, refunds, payouts, reconciliation), campaigns
- *People:* members, small groups/volunteers/attendance, **pastoral care (confidential)**
- *Community:* prayer wall, comment/testimony moderation, sending notifications
- *Administration:* analytics, settings, staff & roles

Church admins have every permission. Keep *pastoral care* to your pastoral team only: notes and counseling are encrypted and private.

## Step 4: Set up giving

**Settings → Giving channels**

1. Turn on the methods you accept: **MTN MoMo**, **Airtel Money**, **Iotec**, **card** (for diaspora members), **bank transfer**. Cash is always available for recording.
2. Enter your merchant or collection details for each channel, and your bank account for payouts.
3. Make a small test gift yourself. You should get the PIN prompt on your phone, then a receipt by email and SMS.
4. Share your giving link (`faithfellows.com/give/your-church`). Each campaign also has a printable poster with a QR code.

Each giver gets an instant numbered receipt. Finance staff see gifts live under **Finance**, record cash and envelope offerings, reconcile bank statements (CSV import) and track payouts.

**Campaigns** (building fund, missions, welfare…) show a progress ring, milestones (25/50/75/100%), a leaderboard (opt-in), pledges and updates. Create one under **Campaigns → New campaign**.

## Step 5: Your first sermon and live service

**Upload a sermon:** go to **Sermons → New sermon**. Add the title, preacher and scripture, then upload the audio (MP3) or paste a YouTube link. Group sermons into series. Members can listen, download for offline use and share.

**Go live with OBS Studio (free):**

1. **Live streams → Schedule** a service. Subscribers are notified when you go live.
2. Open the stream, copy the **Server URL** and **Stream key**. Never share the key.
3. In OBS, open **Settings → Stream**, choose **Service: Custom**, and paste both values.
4. In OBS, open **Settings → Output** and choose a bitrate. Use 2500 kbps for good internet, or 800–1200 kbps for slower connections. Set the keyframe interval to 2 seconds.
5. Click **Start Streaming**. Your service appears live on the church page, the member app and your website within about 20 seconds.

Prefer YouTube or Facebook Live? Choose **External URL** when scheduling and paste the link instead.

After the service, publish the recording as a sermon with one click.

## Step 6: Grow your community

- **Announcements:** target everyone, members, staff, a branch or a small group, with optional email/SMS.
- **Send notification:** the composer reaches a segment by in-app message, email, push, SMS or WhatsApp. It shows the SMS cost before you send. Use **Urgent** only for genuine emergencies, because it overrides members' quiet hours.
- **Events:** RSVPs, paid tickets with QR codes, and check-in from any phone at the door.
- **Prayer wall and testimonies:** members share requests (publicly, with the church only, or with pastors only) and pray for one another. You approve testimonies before they appear.
- **Small groups:** create cells and assign leaders. Leaders approve members, take attendance and message their group from their own phone.
- **Volunteers:** build teams (ushers, choir, media, children's church) and a rota. Volunteers confirm or decline, and get a reminder the day before.
- **Attendance:** print **Today's QR code** and put it at the entrance. Members scan it to check in. Record guests and children's headcounts manually.
- **Pastoral care:** see birthdays, anniversaries and follow-ups, plus "We miss you" lists of regular attenders absent for 3 weeks, home visits, welfare support and the discipleship pathway.

## Step 7: Your website

**Settings → Website & branding** builds your site at `your-church.faithfellows.com` from blocks (hero, live, about, service times, sermons, events, campaigns, giving, prayer, contact). Drag blocks to reorder them and switch them on or off.

Want your own domain, like `www.yourchurch.org`? Request it under **Billing & plan** (UGX 60,000/year), and we'll guide you through the DNS setup.

## Step 8: Invite your members

- Open **Invite link** (Church admin → People) for your church's own link, for example `faithfellows.com/join/FF47KM`. Share it on WhatsApp or Facebook, or print the **QR poster** for the noticeboard and the bulletin. Whoever opens it is taken to the app (or to its download first), and as soon as they create their account they follow your church. The page shows how many people opened the link and how many joined through it. The code is six characters (FF, two digits and two letters) so it is easy to say and to type; if a link ever has to stop working, **Make a new code** retires it.
- Announce FaithFellows on Sunday and show the QR code to your church page.
- Members create one free account and tap **Subscribe**. They choose which alerts they want (live, sermons, events, giving, prayer) and on which channels.
- Turn on **member approval** under **Settings → Church profile** if you want to approve each new member.
- Members can install FaithFellows on their phone like an app: **Add to Home screen**.
