Part A

The idea

What FaithFellows is, the problem it solves, who it helps and how it sustains itself.

1 · FaithFellows on one page

FaithFellows gives every church its own digital home, and every member their church family in their pocket, at a price every church can afford.

The problem

Most churches reach people only on Sunday. Radio and TV are expensive, giving is mostly cash with no records, members are scattered across WhatsApp groups and notebooks, and a custom website or app is out of reach for a small church.

The solution

One platform with three parts: a mobile app for members (Bible, hymns, sermons, prayer, events, games, giving), a church portal for leaders (content, people, money, records) and a control room for the platform owner. Giving is by mobile money.

Who pays

Churches pay a small monthly fee (from UGX 60,000). Members use the app free. Every church gets its own website, hosting and support included.

What changes

People hear and read the Word daily, not only on Sunday. Leaders see who follows them and how they are doing. Treasurers get receipts, records and reports. Givers are thanked by email within minutes.

“God loves a cheerful giver.” — 2 Corinthians 9:7. FaithFellows makes giving as easy as a prompt on the phone, and makes sure every gift is thanked, recorded and accounted for.

2 · The problem

Churches in Uganda and across Africa do remarkable work with very little, and they face the same handful of problems. These are the situations FaithFellows was built for.

3 · The solution

FaithFellows replaces the patchwork with one connected system. A church signs up, is approved, and immediately has a website, an admin portal and a presence in the app that its members download.

📱

The member app

Home, Bible, Community and Give tabs plus Me. Sermons, live services, devotionals, prayer wall, events and tickets, radio, podcasts, a hymn book, games, people to walk with, and giving.

🏛️

The church portal

A website dashboard for pastors and staff: publish content, schedule events, go live, manage people and groups, record giving, run fundraisers, see statistics and send notifications.

🧭

The control room

For the platform owner: approve churches, set the plans and fee, watch payments and activity, decide which features each church sees, send inspiration, and keep the platform healthy.

What makes it work in the real world

  • Mobile money first. A gift is a prompt on the phone approved with the PIN, through MTN and Airtel.
  • Light and offline. The Bible and the hymn book can be saved on the phone and read without data.
  • Faith-based by design. Podcasts are approved before listing, hymns always carry their credits, and contact details are never shared between members.
  • Affordable. The church fee covers the website, hosting, storage, support and the app presence.
  • Measurable. Leaders and treasurers see growth, engagement and giving in numbers they can act on.

5 · The need, and why now

  • Mobile money is how people already pay. The missing piece was connecting it to church giving with receipts and records.
  • Phones are how people already read and listen. Members expect their church to be there, as everything else is.
  • Churches already rely on digital tools, but in disconnected ways (chat groups, photos of notebooks). A single system turns that effort into records and insight.
  • Trust and accountability matter more than ever. Donors give more freely when they are thanked, can see receipts and know their church keeps clean accounts.
  • Local relevance is rare. Most church software is built for other markets. FaithFellows is built in Lira, priced in shillings and designed for low data.
The invitation
Churches, denominations and fellowships are invited to pilot FaithFellows: a church can be live in a day, with its members following it by QR code or a link.

9 · A church’s journey

  1. Apply. A church fills in the registration form on the website (name, denomination, place, contact).
  2. Approval. The platform owner reviews and approves it; the church gets its website address, its admin portal, a set of roles (Editor, Finance, Media, Events, Moderator) and a starting list of giving reasons.
  3. Set up. The setup checklist guides the church: profile, logo and cover, service times, staff, giving channels (mobile money), first announcement.
  4. Members follow. People find the church in the app’s directory, or scan its QR code or open its link, and follow it.
  5. Publish and engage. Sermons, devotionals, announcements, events, live services, prayer requests, groups and reading plans reach followers on their phones; notifications go only to the church’s followers.
  6. Give and record. Members give by mobile money; the church sees every gift grouped by reason, plus fundraisers and records.
  7. Pay the monthly fee. The portal always shows the next payment day; reminders go out a week and a day before.
Staying active
A church that goes completely quiet (no staff sign-in, nothing published, no new followers) for 30 days gets a warning by email and in the portal. If it is still quiet 14 days later it is paused, and the platform owner can open it again. Nothing is deleted.

11 · How giving works

  1. The church decides what people can give for. Each church writes its own list of giving reasons (Tithe, Offering, Building fund, a thanksgiving…). It also starts fundraisers with a goal and a closing date.
  2. The member chooses. They enter an amount; their phone number from their profile is already filled in below it; they pick the reason (or a fundraiser) and press Give.
  3. A prompt appears on the phone. They enter their mobile money PIN to approve; the amount is deducted from their mobile money.
  4. The system confirms the payment with the mobile money company, marks the gift as received, and writes the receipt.
  5. Everyone is updated at once: the member gets a receipt (PDF) and a thank-you email; the fundraiser’s progress moves; the treasurer’s records and the finance team’s alerts update.
  6. The church sees it clustered: by reason, fundraiser and payment method, with fees and the net received, and can download the ledger as CSV or PDF.
Honest records
Every gift keeps the name of the reason as it was given, so renaming or removing a reason later never changes past records. Pending, failed and refunded gifts are shown separately and never counted as received.

12 · How the monthly fee works

A church’s fee keeps its portal and its place in the app running. The amount is its plan price, set by the platform owner.

WhenWhat happens
A week before the payment dayAn invoice is prepared and the church’s owner and admins get an email: the amount, the payment date and a Pay button.
The day beforeA second reminder email.
On the payment dayThe church pays with mobile money: it enters a phone number, gets a prompt and approves it with the PIN.
When paidA thank-you email with the next payment date; the “paid until” date moves forward a month.
If it is lateReminders continue. After the grace days (14 by default) the church is paused until it pays; paying brings it back at once.

13 · Privacy, safety and trust

Who can see what

  • Each church’s data is kept separate from every other church.
  • Staff have roles with exact permissions: only finance staff see giving, and only care staff see pastoral notes (which even platform staff cannot read).
  • Members’ own statistics are private; churches only see anonymous totals.

Safety between members

  • Meeting other believers is for adults who have given their date of birth.
  • Members choose whether to be found; only an approximate distance is ever shown, never a place.
  • Phone numbers, emails and links cannot be sent between members; blocking works both ways; reports go to moderators.

Security

  • Passwords and secrets are stored encrypted.
  • Sign-in with Google, or email with a reset code that expires in 15 minutes.
  • Every important action is written to an audit log.
  • Limits on repeated attempts protect sign-in and payments.

Respect for content

  • Hymns carry their credits and only the platform owner adds them.
  • Licensed Bible text (the ESV) is never stored or downloaded.
  • Podcasts are approved before they are listed; the audio stays with its owner.
Part C

User guide

Step by step, with the screens, for members, church leaders and the platform owner.

14 · Members: getting started

Install the FaithFellows app on your phone and open it. The first screen offers two ways in.

  1. Press Continue with Google and choose your Google account, or press Continue with email and type your email and password.
  2. New here? Press Create an account, fill in your name, email and password and accept the terms.
  3. Forgot your password? Press Forgot your password?, enter your email, and type the 6-digit code we send you with a new password. Check the spam folder if it does not arrive. You can ask for a new code after 45 seconds.
  4. When the phone asks, press Allow for notifications so you get news from your church.
  5. Find your church: open Community → Find churches, or scan the church’s QR code, and press Follow. You can follow up to three churches.
Add your phone number once
Your profile asks for your phone number. It is saved there and fills in mobile money for you every time you give.

15 · Members: the Home screen

Home is your daily starting point.

  • When the app opens, a short greeting (“Good morning, Grace”) shows for a few seconds above the slider.
  • The slider at the top shows what is happening at your church: live services, events, sermons, fundraisers. Tap a slide to open it.
  • Explore opens radio, podcasts, hymns, sermons, events, prayer, giving and games. The number on Prayer is how many requests are waiting for you to pray.
  • Inspiration is a short, strong message for the morning, mid-morning or evening. Tap the share icon to send it to someone.
  • Highlights slide by themselves: the live service, urgent notices, announcements, the devotional, fundraisers and the next event.
  • Verse of the day, Games, Top podcasts and Sermons finish the page. Pull down to refresh.

16 · Members: Bible

  1. Open the Bible tab. The chip under the title shows your version; tap it to change to another (ESV when available, KJV, WEB, WEBBE, ASV, BBE, Darby, Douay-Rheims).
  2. Books are numbered in order, Genesis 1 to Revelation 66. Choose Old or New Testament, tap a book and then a chapter.
  3. Type a reference such as John 3:16 or Psalm 23 in the search box to jump straight to it.
  4. In a chapter, tap verses to select them, then highlight in one of four colours, copy, write a note or share. Sharing makes a neat scripture card.
  5. Use Reading plans and My notes from the Bible tab. The cloud button saves a version on your phone to read without data (not available for the ESV).
Your reading counts
The minutes you spend in the Bible are added to your Bible ring on your profile, privately.

17 · Members: Games

Games are Bible quizzes you play with friends. There are 25 ready-made games of 10 questions: Creation, Moses, David, the Prophets, Women of the Bible, the Christmas story, Parables, Miracles, Acts, Paul, Revelation, Books of the Bible, Bible lands, and Faith in Uganda among them.

  1. Open Games from Home. Filter by category and press Start on a game.
  2. You become the host and a player. Press Share PIN to send the 6-digit code by WhatsApp or SMS, or Invite people and tap Invite next to the names of people you have played with or are connected to.
  3. Your friends get a notification and a push; they tap it, or enter the PIN under Join a friend’s game.
  4. When everyone is in, press Start game. Everyone is told it has started. Each question is open for 10 seconds; fast, correct answers score more.
  5. At the end you see the podium and the scoreboard.

18 · Members: Grow your circle

  1. Open Community → Grow your circle. The list shows names only (no ages) and a short reason, such as “Both like Worship & Prayer”.
  2. For you ranks people by what you share: interests, podcasts, hymns, scriptures, church, town and age. My church, Near me and Everyone are other views.
  3. In Near me, press Allow location once, then pick a distance (5 to 200 km). Churches around you are listed first. Others only see roughly how far away you are, never where.
  4. Press Say Hey 👋 to ask to connect. Once they accept, you can message each other. To be found yourself, add your birthday and choose to be listed in your profile.
For adults only
Connecting is for members aged 18 and over who have added their date of birth. Phone numbers and links cannot be sent in messages.

19 · Members: giving and fundraisers

  1. Open the Give tab. If you follow one church it appears at the top as “Your church”; press Give.
  2. Type the amount, or tap a quick amount. Your mobile money number (from your profile) is right below it. A note reminds you that a prompt will be sent to that phone.
  3. Choose what it is for: the reasons your church has listed. Under More options you can give anonymously, repeat the gift weekly, monthly or quarterly, or add a note.
  4. Press Give. On your phone, enter your mobile money PIN when the prompt appears. The amount is deducted from your mobile money.
  5. You get a receipt in the app (Give → My gifts) and a thank-you email with a PDF receipt.
  6. Fundraisers: open one from Highlights or the Give tab, press Donate, enter the amount and confirm your number. The page shows how much is raised and the Givers, most recent first.

20 · Members: your profile and statistics

  • Complete your profile. The page asks for your phone number first, then your photo, birthday, town, a line about you and your interests. The percentage shows how far along you are; every detail helps with suggestions and with giving.
  • My churches lists the churches you follow; tap one to open it.
  • My progress has three rings for this week or this month: Bible (minutes reading), Listening (radio and podcasts) and Sharing (verses and inspiring messages you shared). They fill against gentle goals: about 10 minutes of Bible a day, an hour of listening and a few shares a week.
  • Your numbers shows your streak of days, chapters read, listening time, shares, highlights and prayers. See all opens every figure, for the week, the month or all time.

These figures are only for you. Nobody else sees them, and churches only ever see anonymous totals.

23 · Church leaders: money and records

  1. Giving reasons (Finance → Giving reasons): add what members can give for. Choose which platform category it counts as; switch one off to hide it from members.
  2. Campaigns: create a fundraiser with a goal, a story, a picture and a closing date. Members donate from the app; progress and the list of givers update live.
  3. Record cash: use “Record cash / bank gift” so offline gifts appear in the same records and receipts.
  4. Financial records (Finance → Financial records): choose a period; see received, fees and net; the clusters by reason and by payment method; the chart over time; what is pending, refunded or waiting to be paid out; and the ledger of every gift. Filter by reason, fundraiser or method.
  5. Export: download the ledger (CSV), the summary (CSV) or a printable PDF for the board or the accountant.
  6. Refunds and bank transfers: finance staff can refund a gift or confirm a bank transfer; every change is logged.

24 · Church leaders: people and subscribers

Open Subscribers in the portal for the full picture of who follows your church.

  • Followers, how many are waiting for approval and how many left.
  • New followers in the last 7 and 30 days, with growth against the 30 days before, and a chart of the last 12 weeks.
  • Opened the app in the last 30 days (as a percentage and a count), and how many gave in the last 90 days.
  • What followers did in the app (all together): minutes reading the Bible, minutes listening, scriptures and messages shared. Nobody is named.
  • Who they are: by stage (visitor, new member, member, worker, leader), age, gender, town, branch and where they joined from.
  • Newest followers with their join date. Use Members to follow up, message and export.

  • Announcements and posts with photos, pinned or scheduled.

  • Games: host a live quiz for the church.
  • Prayer: approve requests and reply privately.
  • Members and reports at a glance; broadcasts to followers; go live.
  • Giving: this month’s giving clustered by reason, the fees and net, the latest gifts, and the list of giving reasons to add, hide or remove.

I gave but nothing was deducted.The prompt must be approved with your PIN. Open Give → My gifts: a pending gift can be checked from there. Make sure the number is your mobile money number.
Why is my Bible not in the ESV?The ESV needs a licence key on the server. Until then the app uses the King James Version. You can pick another version at any time.
I do not get notifications.Allow notifications for FaithFellows in the phone’s settings; check Me → Notification settings and quiet hours; press “send me a test”.
Who can see my statistics?Only you. Churches see anonymous totals.
Can I follow more than one church?Yes, up to three.
Our church page disappeared.A church that stays quiet for 30 days is warned, then paused 14 days later; an unpaid fee can also pause it. Contact the platform owner; nothing is deleted.
How do we get our members to join?Share the church’s link and QR code from the portal, announce it on Sunday and put the QR code on the screen or the bulletin.
